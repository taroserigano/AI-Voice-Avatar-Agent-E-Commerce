# Returns and refunds

## How long you have

You can return most items within **30 days after delivery**. The 30 days start on the day
the order is delivered, not the day you placed it. Items that haven't been delivered yet
can't be returned; you can track them in **Orders & returns**.

## What you can't return

- **Gift cards** are final sale. They can't be returned or refunded.
- **Fresh and frozen food** (Dairy & Eggs, Fresh Produce, Meat & Seafood, Bakery & Bread,
  and Frozen) isn't sent back. If it arrives damaged, spoiled, or past its date, tell us
  **within 7 days of delivery** and we'll refund it without a return shipment. After 7 days
  we can't refund fresh or frozen food.

## Electronics

Electronics can be returned within 30 days after delivery. They're refunded **after our
warehouse inspects them**, which usually takes 3–5 business days after they arrive.

## When your refund is issued

- **Instant refund:** an item under $25 that doesn't need inspection is refunded as soon
  as the carrier scans your return package (or a Larkspur store scans it at drop-off).
- **Everything else:** refunded after the warehouse receives and inspects the item.

Refunds always go back to **the original payment method**. We don't issue store credit.
Your bank may take a few business days to show the refund.

## How much you get back

- You get back what you paid for the item: its price, minus any discount you received, plus
  the sales tax you paid on it.
- If you return some units of a line (for example 1 of 3 shirts), each unit's share of the
  discount and tax is split evenly in whole cents, and the **last** unit you return takes any
  leftover cent. A $10.00 discount over 3 units is $3.33, $3.33, and $3.34.
- Refunds for a line never add up to more than you paid for that line.
- **Shipping** is refunded only when you return **every item in the order**.
- There's no restocking fee.

## How to return an item

1. Go to **Orders & returns**, choose the order, and choose the item.
2. Tell us why you're returning it and its condition. We'll show you exactly what you'll get
   back before you confirm.
3. Confirm the return. You'll get a prepaid return label.
4. Drop the package with the carrier, or at any Larkspur store's return drop-off. A store
   drop-off counts the same as a carrier scan.

For fresh or frozen food, choose the item and tell us what was wrong; there's nothing to
send back.

## Exceptions

We can't make exceptions to these rules in chat. If you think your situation needs one,
ask for a person and we'll open a support case.
