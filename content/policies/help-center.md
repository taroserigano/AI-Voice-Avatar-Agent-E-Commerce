# Help center

How the Serigan Mart website works: your account, shopping and checkout, your orders, reviews,
stores, and how to reach us. Returns and refunds are in the return and refund policy; delivery
times and costs are in the shipping and delivery policy.

> **Sandbox notice.** Serigan Mart is a demonstration store. No real orders, payments, or
> deliveries take place.

## 1. Your account

### 1.1 Signing up and signing in

Create an account on the **Create your account** page with your name, email, and a password of
at least 8 characters. Everything on the site needs you to be signed in; there's no guest
shopping. You stay signed in on a device for up to 12 hours. To sign out, open the account menu
(**Hi, your name**, at the top right) and choose **Sign out**.

### 1.2 Password, email, and name

The site doesn't have a forgot-password or password-reset page yet, and you can't change your
password, email address, or name yourself. Ask Xena or Steph for a person, and they'll open a
support case.

### 1.3 Closing your account

There's no button to delete or close your account yet. Ask for a person, and they'll open a
support case.

### 1.4 Addresses

There's no saved address book. Each order keeps the address it was delivered to, shown on that
order's page.

### 1.5 Your activity

**Activity**, in the account menu, lists what you've searched for, products you viewed, and your
orders, returns, and reviews.

## 2. Shopping and checkout

### 2.1 Checkout

Checkout is turned off in this demo store. Your cart is saved to your account, but you can't
place an order yet: nothing ships and no money moves. Neither Xena nor Steph can place an order
for you.

### 2.2 Payment methods

There are no live payments while checkout is off. When checkout opens, you'll pay by card on
Stripe's secure checkout page. We don't take PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or cash. We never
see or store full card numbers: an order shows only the card's brand and last four digits.

### 2.3 Promo codes, coupons, and rewards

We don't have promo codes, coupons, a loyalty or rewards program, subscriptions, or price
matching. Prices aren't adjusted after you buy (see the return and refund policy, section 10.2).

### 2.4 Gift cards

Serigan Mart gift cards are digital and delivered by email, in several designs (Serigan Mart,
Happy Birthday, Thank You, and Holiday) and amounts from $15 to $200. They're final sale. While
checkout is off, gift cards can't be bought, redeemed, or checked for a balance.

### 2.5 Your cart

Add items from a product page or with **Buy again** on an order. On the **Cart** page you can
change quantities or **Remove** an item. Each item is limited to 10, and to what's in stock.
Your cart is saved to your account. There's no wishlist, favorites list, or save for later yet.
Xena can tell you what's in your cart but can't change it.

### 2.6 Sales tax

Tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address. In Texas, most grocery food and
gift cards aren't taxed. The sample orders on demo accounts use Texas's 8.25% rate.

## 3. Your orders

### 3.1 Orders & returns

**Orders & returns**, in the account menu, lists every order you've placed, newest first. Use
the tabs (All, In progress, Delivered, Returns) or search by order number or item. An order
number is 8 characters, such as E2EA6BC6.

### 3.2 An order's page

Each order's page shows its status, delivery and tracking, every item with what you paid, the
payment summary (subtotal, discount, shipping, tax, and total), the card's brand and last four
digits, and the address it ships to. Xena can't see your address or card, but they're on the
order's page.

### 3.3 Tracking

The delivery card on an order's page shows the carrier, the tracking number, and every scan:
Ordered, Shipped, Out for delivery, and Delivered. If the carrier reports a problem, it shows
**Delivery issue**. Tracking on demo orders is simulated.

### 3.4 Receipts

Open the order's page and choose **Receipt**. You can print it or save it as a PDF.

### 3.5 Buy again

**Buy again** on an order, or on one of its items, adds those items to your cart again.

### 3.6 Changing or canceling an order

Once an order is placed, you can't change its items or delivery address, and you can't cancel
it yourself. If we can't ship an order on time, you can cancel it for a full refund (return and
refund policy, section 8.1). For anything else about a placed order, ask for a person.

### 3.7 Payment status

An order's payment shows as Paid, Partially refunded, Refunded, or Payment failed. If you think
you were charged incorrectly or twice, ask for a person; disputes are covered in the return and
refund policy, section 13.

## 4. Reviews

### 4.1 Writing a review

On a product's page, go to **Ratings & reviews** and choose **Write a review**. A star rating
from 1 to 5 is required; a title (up to 120 characters) and your review (up to 2,000 characters)
are optional. You can review each product once, and edit or delete your review later. You don't
have to buy a product to review it; reviews from buyers carry a **Verified purchase** badge.

## 5. Stores

### 5.1 Finding a store

We have 10 stores around Dallas–Fort Worth. The **Stores** page finds them by ZIP code or city,
filters by service, and shows each store's address, phone, and hours (Central Time). Holiday
hours may differ. Every store has a return drop-off counter.

### 5.2 Pickup and store stock

Online orders can't be picked up in a store yet; they're always delivered. Product pages show
online stock only, so we can't tell you whether a store has an item on its shelves; call the
store to check.

## 6. Contacting us

### 6.1 Customer service: phone, email, and hours

Call customer service at **(214) 555-0100** or email **help@serigan-mart.example**. Customer
service hours are **7 AM to 10 PM Central Time, every day**. These are demo contact details and
don't reach a real team. You can also reach us here:

- **Xena**, on the **Support** page, by text or voice: returns, refunds, orders, products, and
  policies.
- **Steph**, the **Ask Steph** button on every shopping page: finding products and adding them
  to your cart.

### 6.2 Support cases

When you need a person, Xena or Steph opens a support case for you. You get a reference such as
CS-123456, and a reply within 1 business day. Your cases appear under **Your requests** on the
Support page, as Open, Waiting on you, or Resolved.

## 7. Languages

The website is in English. Xena can reply in the language you write to her in; voice calls are
in English.

## 8. Privacy

We never ask for or store full card numbers, and you should never share a card number,
password, or one-time code in a chat. The **Privacy** page explains what we keep.
