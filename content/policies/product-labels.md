# What our product labels mean

Product pages show labels such as "Organic" or "Fat free". Nutrition amounts are per the
serving size on the product's Nutrition Facts panel.

## Nutrition

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Fat free | Less than 0.5 g of fat per serving (includes "nonfat" and "skim") |
| Low fat | 3 g of fat or less per serving |
| Reduced fat | At least 25% less fat than the regular version (for example 2% milk) |
| Whole milk | Made with full-fat milk |
| Sugar free | Less than 0.5 g of total sugars per serving |
| No added sugar | No sugar or syrup is added; the product may still have natural sugars, such as the lactose in milk |
| Unsweetened | No sugar and no sweeteners of any kind are added |
| Low sugar | 6 g of total sugars or less per serving |
| Zero calorie | Fewer than 5 calories per serving |
| High protein | 10 g of protein or more per serving |
| High fiber | 5 g of fiber or more per serving |
| Keto friendly | 5 g of net carbs (carbs minus fiber) or less per serving |
| Low sodium | 140 mg of sodium or less per serving |

## Diet and allergens

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Lactose free | The lactose is removed or broken down. **It can still contain milk** (lactose-free milk is still cow's milk), so it isn't safe for a milk allergy. |
| Dairy free | Contains no milk ingredients at all |
| Vegan | No animal ingredients: no meat, fish, dairy, eggs, honey, or gelatin |
| Plant based | Made mainly from plants, as an alternative to an animal product |
| Gluten free | Made without wheat, barley, or rye |
| Nut free | Contains no peanuts or tree nuts |

Every food page lists its ingredients and the major allergens it contains (milk, egg, fish,
shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, sesame). If you have a severe allergy, always
check the package itself as well.

## Sourcing

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| Organic | Produced to USDA organic standards |
| Grass fed | From animals fed a grass-based diet |
| Cage free / Free range / Pasture raised | How the hens were raised, from indoor cage-free barns to time outdoors on pasture |
| Wild caught | Fish caught in the wild, not farm raised |
| Texas grown | Grown in Texas |
