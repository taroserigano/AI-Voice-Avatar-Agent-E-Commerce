# Shipping and delivery

## 1. Shipping cost

Standard shipping is **$5.99**, and **free** when your order is $35 or more after discounts and
before tax. There's one shipping speed: standard.

## 2. Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the **United States only**. We don't ship to other countries (Canada,
Mexico, or anywhere else) or offer international shipping.

## 3. How long delivery takes

Orders ship from our warehouse, usually within 1 business day, and arrive in 2–5 business days.
Each order's page shows its arriving-by date, carrier, and tracking number.

## 4. Order status

You can follow every order in **Orders & returns**. Its status is one of:

- **Preparing**: we've received payment and haven't shipped it yet.
- **In progress**: it's being packed or it's on the way.
- **Delivered**: the carrier marked it delivered.
- **Canceled**: the order was canceled and won't ship.

## 5. Store pickup

Stores don't hold online orders for pickup yet; online orders are always delivered.

## 6. Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address. Most grocery food and gift
cards aren't taxed in Texas.
