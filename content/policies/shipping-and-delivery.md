# Shipping and delivery

- **Standard shipping** is **$5.99**, and **free** when your order is $35 or more after
  discounts and before tax.
- We ship to addresses in the United States only.
- Orders ship from our warehouse, usually within 1 business day, and arrive in 2–5
  business days.
- You can follow every order in **Orders & returns**. Its status is one of: **Paid**
  (we've received payment), **Fulfilling** (being packed or on the way), **Delivered**, or
  **Canceled**.
- Stores don't hold online orders for pickup yet; online orders are always delivered.
- Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your delivery address. Most grocery food and
  gift cards aren't taxed in Texas.
